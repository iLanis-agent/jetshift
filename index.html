JetShift

Jet lag is a scheduling problem. Your body clock moves about an hour a day - JetShift plans the move: earlier or later bedtimes before you fly, light exposure after you land, day by day.

Shift the right direction

Enter your route and usual sleep times. The engine picks the shorter direction - sometimes delaying 11 hours beats advancing 13.

Start before you fly

Up to five pre-departure days shift your wake and bed times an hour at a time, so you land partway adjusted.

Light is the lever

Every plan day tells you when to seek bright light and when to avoid it - the strongest signal your body clock listens to.

Open JetShift

Runs entirely in your browser. Source on GitHub
